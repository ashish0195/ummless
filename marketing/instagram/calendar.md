# The first four weeks

Three grid posts a week (Monday, Wednesday, Friday) build the 3 × 3 grid in [preview.png](preview.png) by the end
of week 3. Carousels and the reel follow in week 4, once the grid has its pattern. Stories go out on the other
weekdays.

Start with these times, then move to what *Insights → Audience → Most active times* shows after two weeks:

- **US audience:** 11 AM – 1 PM Eastern on weekdays.
- **India audience:** 7–9 PM IST on weekdays, a little later on Sundays.

After week 4, aim for one save-worthy carousel and one reel a week, and keep *Today's prompt* in stories. Reply to
every comment in the first hour after posting.

## What to watch

- **Saves and shares** per post, more than likes: they're what tells Instagram a post is useful.
- **Profile visits → follows**, the share of visitors who follow.
- **Poll taps** on *Today's prompt*: the stories people answer are the prompts worth turning into posts.

Don't buy followers or use engagement pods. They distort these numbers and the audience never converts.
