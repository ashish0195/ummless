# Reel scripts

Short talking-head reels you can film on a phone, by a window, in one take. Each is under 30 seconds. Put the
words on screen as captions (Instagram's *Captions* sticker does it automatically): most people watch without
sound.

Rules: one idea per reel, the hook in the first two seconds, and no made-up results or testimonials.

## 1. The 60-second challenge

- **Hook (on screen):** "Explain your job to a 10-year-old. You have 60 seconds."
- **You:** "Try it. Out loud. No jargon." *(Pause, then try it yourself, honestly, stumbles included.)*
- **You:** "Harder than it sounds, right? The trick: one everyday picture, start to finish."
- **End card:** reels/frames/5.png. Caption: "Comment your one-line version 👇"

## 2. Stop starting with "So, basically…"

- **Hook:** "The first five words of your update decide whether anyone listens."
- **Before:** "So, basically, I looked into a few things this week, and…"
- **After:** "We should push the launch by a week. Here's why."
- **You:** "Point first. Then the why. Try it in your next meeting."

## 3. How to enter a GD (India)

- **Hook:** "You don't have to speak first in a GD. You have to speak *early*."
- **You:** "One clear point in the first few minutes. Not 'there are many aspects to this topic'."
- **Example:** "Hybrid wins: two office days for teamwork, three at home for focus."
- **You:** "Then build on someone by name. That's how moderators notice you."

## 4. Your accent is fine

- **Hook:** "Nobody needs to fix your accent."
- **You:** "What people remember is whether they understood your point. So work on that: lead with it, keep
  sentences short, pause before the important bit."
- **You:** "Hinglish in the team call? Bilkul. Clear is what counts."

## 5. The screen recording

Film the app instead of yourself: today's practice → speak → the feedback. Speed up the waiting, keep it under
20 seconds, and end on the "Next time" card. Use the demo account, so no real person's data is on screen.
