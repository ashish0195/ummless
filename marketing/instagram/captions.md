## Carousels

### 5 ways to sound clearer in meetings

```text
Save this for your next meeting.

1. Lead with the point, then the why.
2. One idea per sentence.
3. Pause before the line that matters.
4. Name the next step: who, what, by when.
5. Hand the floor over with a real question.

Pick one and try it today.

#meetings #communicationskills #worktips #careeradvice
```

Alt text: Slide 1, "5 ways to sound clearer in meetings". Slides 2–6 each show a tip with an "Instead of" and a "Try" example. Slide 7, "Practise one of these a day" above the app.

### How to stand out in a GD (India)

```text
Placement season? Save this.

1. Enter early with one clear point.
2. Build on someone by name.
3. Add something new: a fact, an example or a trade-off.
4. Bring in the quiet person.
5. Offer the summary near the end.

None of these need you to be the loudest in the room.

#groupdiscussion #placements #campusplacement #mba
```
